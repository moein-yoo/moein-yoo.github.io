About

Engineering across theory and hardware.

I am an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at Sharif University of Technology. My work spans graph and time-vertex signal processing, optimization, neural signal analysis, analog electronics, RF systems, and antenna design.

I am particularly interested in problems where mathematical models interact with real-world signals and physical systems: from graph-based forecasting and nonconvex optimization to neural recordings, analog circuits, and high-frequency antennas.
